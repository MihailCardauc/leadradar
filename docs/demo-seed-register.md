## Notes for maintainers
- `text` is the rendered paragraph around the quote with markdown markup removed. `hash = sha256(text)` is precomputed and re-verified by the unit test.
- `quality` follows `qualityRubric(sourceType, quoteValid=true, companyNamed=true, dateValid)` from `src/server/providers.ts`: newsroom 0.9, news 0.8, career_page without a date 0.75.
- `eventKey = <companyId>:<slug>:<date|unknown>`. Rows from the same event share a key (CFR SA tender across two services; AQUILA filing across two questions), so republication never stacks.
- The firmographics shown are only what the pages state. Revenue given in RON (AQUILA, eMAG) is not converted and stays `null`. Headcounts marked "peste/over" are lower bounds.
