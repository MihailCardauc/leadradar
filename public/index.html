The problem

Researching one company is a task. Researching a hundred is a full-time job.

Any experienced salesperson can assess one company. At a hundred, the method breaks: tenders, job postings, news and annual reports for each, judged by experience, ranked by hand. About 25 minutes per company, 83 hours a month for two hundred, and the first signals are already stale when the list is finished. LeadRadar runs the same chain on every company, every day, and shows the reasoning behind each one.

ICP criteria

Market, industry, company size, geography, operational complexity. Mandatory criteria gate; preferred ones add fit.

Signal questions per service

"Is the company hiring RPA developers or process-excellence roles?" — high, medium or low importance, positive or negative polarity.

Negative rules

Penalties (a certified internal SOC: −15) or gates (a competitor bidding on the same tenders: excluded).

Scoring logic

Weights, half-lives and the 0.35 / 0.65 balance are per-service settings, saved as reviewed versions, never applied silently.

AI & scoring

The algorithm does the reading and the maths. You decide.

P = clamp(0.35 · F + 0.65 · R − N, 0, 100)
F · ICP fit

Weighted match on confirmed criteria. Unknowns earn nothing and cost nothing; the denominator is never shrunk to the data found.

R · signal relevance

Per rule: weight × quality × recency, with d = 2^(−age/half-life). Quality comes from a versioned rubric, not a free-text probability.

N · explicit penalties

Only configured negative rules. Missing information never penalises; a failed mandatory condition is a gate, not a penalty.

Prediction layer

Buying stage, momentum and a 45–90-day opportunity window from signal sequences, labelled as heuristic estimates until calibrated on outcomes.

Our own scoring algorithm processes every company the same way: the AI reads the sources and explains what it found, the versioned formula turns that into a priority, and every contribution stays visible on screen. Nothing is decided for you. You see the evidence, you approve, edit or reject, and each of your decisions proposes a better weight for the next version, which you approve as well. Swap the language model and the score does not move; that is what makes it yours to trust or contest.

Trust & compliance

Knowing a company is ready is not the same as permission to write to it.

So LeadRadar identifies, enriches, personalises and recommends, and then stops. A governance layer sits between the signal and the message, a person approves every action, and the data stays in Europe.

GDPR

Privacy by design

Company-level facts by default. Where a professional contact is used, the record carries its source, lawful basis (a documented Legitimate Interest Assessment), retention and suppression status. Objections are honoured globally, before any draft is generated.

EU AI Act

Advisory, not automated decisions

B2B account prioritisation is not a listed high-risk use. Scores stay advisory; human review is mandatory in the architecture, not just in policy. AI involvement is labelled in the interface and documented (model, data, limits, oversight).

EU hosting

Data stays in the EU

Target environment: Hetzner, Falkenstein (Germany), with a self-hosted, EU-resident language model option so no CRM or accounting data leaves the EU. Region, sub-processors and transfers are confirmed contractually and technically in the pilot.

ISO 27001 · in progress

Security programme

Tenant isolation with row-level security, purpose-based access, audit trail per decision, token rotation and revocation, incident response with 72-hour authority notification. Certification is under way, not claimed.

Data protection. LeadRadar processes company-level data (name, registry ID, sector, turnover), which is not personal data. Professional contact data, where used, is processed under legitimate interest with a documented assessment, minimised to name, role, professional address and company, with a lawful basis and retention recorded per record and a global suppression list checked before any draft is generated. Rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection have an operational flow; objection to direct marketing is honoured immediately and across all workflows. A Data Processing Agreement is available to customers on request; a DPIA is assessed by actual risk under Article 35 and completed before commercial launch. Sending commercial messages follows the rules of the recipient's country (Legea 506/2004 in Romania, Legea 195/2024 in Moldova); LeadRadar prepares, a person sends.
AI transparency. Scores and drafts are generated by an AI system from public data and are labelled as such; every decision includes a source link for human verification. B2B account prioritisation is not a listed high-risk use under the EU AI Act; the system is advisory, produces no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effect on persons, and is not to be repurposed for evaluating individuals in recruitment, credit or similar contexts.
Hosting and security. EU hosting (Hetzner, Germany) with an EU-resident model option is the target environment and is confirmed contractually and technically per customer during the pilot. ISO/IEC 27001 certification is in progress. We do not present GDPR compliance, AI Act conformity or ISO certification as achieved results until they are verified; we describe the controls we operate.
