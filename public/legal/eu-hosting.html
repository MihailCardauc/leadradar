Transfers

No routine transfer outside the EU/EEA. Any exception is listed per sub-processor with its legal mechanism (adequacy decision or SCCs) on the Security page.

Moldova

Customers and users in Moldova access an EU-hosted service; company-level data collected from Moldovan public sources is processed in the EU under Legea 195/2024 rules for cross-border transfer, which we document in the DPA.

Verification

Region, sub-processors, retention and transfers are confirmed in writing in the pilot agreement and can be verified by the customer's security team before go-live.
